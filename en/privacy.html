Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

This policy explains what information the business "Lipshtat Tal – Sports Therapy and Sports Injury Rehabilitation" (licensed sole proprietor, 6 Pirchei Aviv St., Tel Aviv-Yafo, Israel) collects, how it is used, and what rights you have regarding it.

Information we collect

  • Contact details: name, phone number and email address.
  • Information you provide for treatment or training: goals, injury history, pain, limitations and training progress.
  • The content of messages exchanged with us, including on WhatsApp.
  • Class and session booking details.

How we use it

  • Providing treatment, building training programs and tracking your progress.
  • Scheduling sessions and sending reminders, updates and service messages, including on WhatsApp.
  • Responding to inquiries and improving our service.
  • Meeting legal obligations, such as bookkeeping.

WhatsApp messages

Service messages, such as class or session reminders, are sent through Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform. Messages are sent only to clients and trainees who have provided their phone number. You can stop receiving messages at any time by replying "STOP" (or "הסר") or by contacting us.

Sharing information

We do not sell your information or share it with third parties for marketing. It is stored and processed by service providers that help us run the business, and only to the extent needed: our studio management and booking system, cloud hosting services, Meta for WhatsApp messaging, and our accounting software. Information will be disclosed to authorities only when required by law.

Security and retention

Information is kept on secure systems, and access is limited to those who need it to provide the service. We keep information for as long as it is needed for treatment or training, or as required by law.

Your rights

You may request to access, correct or delete the information we hold about you, subject to applicable law. To make a request, contact us by email or phone as listed below.
