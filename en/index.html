About

I'm Tal Lipshtat. I've been coaching for 11 years, and I'm a sports therapist, massage therapist and yoga teacher. I'm a former football player and have trained in the gym since my teens.

My approach is simple: every pain has a source. A stiff neck, for example, can point to weakness or limited mobility somewhere else entirely. The goal is not only to make the pain go away, but to restore full function, strength and performance.
